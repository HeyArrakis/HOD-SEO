## 17. Data limitations (read before using this dataset)

1. **This is 1,000 of 518,487 keywords DataForSEO's database associates with this domain** (`total_count` in the raw response = 518,487; `items_count` returned = 1,000). This pull captured **0.19%** of the available pool, ordered by DataForSEO's default relevance ranking (`relevance,desc`). It is a top-slice sample, not the full universe — a materially different-looking set could result from a different `order_by`, `filters`, or `offset`/`offset_token` value. No further paid calls were made this stage per instruction; if a fuller pull is wanted later, that requires a new estimate and approval.
2. **Significant topical noise from word-level polysemy.** A large share of returned keywords use "diamond" or "ring" in a sense that has nothing to do with jewelry: Minecraft mining (*netherite ingot*, *what is the best y level for diamonds*, *can you mine diamonds with a copper pickaxe*, *mcpe diamond*), industrial abrasives/tooling (*mk diamond core bit*, *diamond z grinders*, *dlc diamond-like carbon*, *rolled ring forgings*, *epdm o rings*), home-security-camera brand comparisons (*vivint vs ring*, *wyze vs ring* — "Ring" the doorbell brand), video games (*is tiffany jewelry worth it in cyberpunk 2077*, *kcd2*), and other unrelated domains (*diamond barre*, a fitness brand; *selenite wand*). 745 of 1,000 keywords contain the substring "diamond" at all, and the classifier's category tags (§6–7) do not distinguish these from genuine jewelry demand — that separation currently has to be done by reading keyword text.
3. **Category metadata is not discriminating.** All 1,000 rows carry the same 4-category bundle (Apparel / Jewelry / Precious & Semi-Precious Gems & Gemstone Jewelry / Diamond Jewelry); only 2 more specific categories (Rings, Engagement Rings) appear at all, each on a single keyword. Category data cannot be used to filter this dataset for relevance.
4. **Explicit product-category terms are almost absent** (0 "engagement ring", 0 "wedding band", 0 "necklace"/"pendant", 0 "bracelet" — see §8). This is a property of the relevance algorithm applied to this specific domain at this point in time, not evidence that search demand for these categories doesn't exist.
5. **Metrics are DataForSEO model snapshots** (`keyword_info.last_updated_time` values from within the last few weeks of September 2026 for most rows), not a live SERP re-check. Search volume, CPC, competition, and keyword difficulty are reported as returned — none were invented, recalculated, or smoothed.
6. **`keyword_difficulty` is often 0 or null** for very low-volume/low-competition rows in this pull — per DataForSEO's own definition this reflects the top-10 organic ranking difficulty signal, not a guarantee of easy ranking, and is not treated as one here.

## Cross-check: current HOD rankings vs. broader keyword universe

Comparing this 1,000-keyword universe against the 28 keywords HOD currently ranks for ([`03_hod_ranked_keywords.csv`](03_hod_ranked_keywords.csv)):

- **Exact-string overlap: 3 of HOD's 28 current ranking keywords** also appear in this 1,000-row universe pull: `hpht lab grown diamonds`, `cvd diamond vs lab grown`, `lab grown diamonds cvd vs hpht`.
- **25 of HOD's 28 current ranking keywords do not appear** in this particular 1,000-row slice of the universe (they may well exist elsewhere in the remaining 517,487 unpulled keywords — this is not evidence they're absent from the broader universe, only from this sample).
- **Overlap percentage: ~10.7%** (3/28), by exact string match against this sample only.
- Framed the other way: of the 1,000 keywords in this broader-universe pull, all but those 3 are **keywords in the broader universe without a current HOD ranking** — the great majority of this sample. This is stated as a fact about sample composition, not an "opportunity" claim, per project rules.
- This low overlap is expected and not itself meaningful: Stage 3 pulled HOD's actual ranking positions (a small, page-3+-only set), while this stage pulled a relevance-ranked top-1,000 slice of a 518K-keyword domain-adjacent pool. The two datasets are different views by design; a low overlap does not indicate a data error.

## Site coverage cross-check

Comparing this universe against [`site/01_site_inventory.csv`](../../site/01_site_inventory.csv) / [`01_site_inventory.md`](../../site/01_site_inventory.md):

**B. HOD has products/content but weak/no current rankings for the matched topics:**
- Diamond terminology/quality — color and clarity grade comparison queries (D vs E, VS vs SI, etc., 52 keywords, 15,420 volume) touch ground the existing "4Cs" blog article covers only at a general level; no dedicated grade-comparison content exists per Stage 1.
- Diamond shapes/cuts — HOD has a dedicated "best diamond shapes" blog article and shape-specific PDPs, but the shape-related keywords actually surfaced in this pull are mostly tangential (pearl-vs-diamond comparisons) rather than direct shape-selection queries; genuine alignment here is weaker than the raw count suggests.

- Simulated/alternative-diamond and competitor-brand comparisons (Ritani, Diamond Nexus, Agape, Tiffany, Iced London, Robin Hood Diamonds) — no equivalent content exists anywhere in the Stage 1 inventory.
