Also checked:
- **Live verification of 69 competitor-linking pages:** none contains "House of Diams" text or a link to houseofdiams.com.
- **DataForSEO C1:** HOD's only 21 referring domains are spam-pattern pages. The 4 `…hub.shop` pages use "houseofdiams.com" as anchor text, so they are linked spam, not editorial mentions.

### Limitations
- A web search engine is not a mention-monitoring index. It matched near-variants ("House of Diamonds") aggressively, and pages without exact-string indexing could be missed.
- Social platforms, marketplaces (Etsy, Amazon), press-release wires and Indian-language sources were not searched exhaustively.
- **Paid option (not run; needs separate approval):** DataForSEO's Content Analysis API can search a citation index for a brand string. Its pricing was not checked in this session; a cost would have to be quoted before asking for approval.

### Entity-clarity implication
"House of Diamonds" is a crowded name: at least five unrelated jewelers use it. Journalists and AI systems can easily confuse it with "House of Diams". Every pitch and profile should use the exact spelling **House of Diams** and link to houseofdiams.com. Once F-C1 is confirmed, consistent company facts across profiles will help disambiguation. This is an industry-practice judgment, not a Google-stated rule.
